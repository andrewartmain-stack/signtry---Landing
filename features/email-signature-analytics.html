What email signature analytics measure

Your signature goes out in every email you send, often hundreds a week. Email signature tracking shows whether anyone acts on it. The SignTry dashboard counts link clicks on every link in your signature and breaks them down so you can see what is working.

How to use your signature data

  1. Move your best link first. If “Book a call” gets three times the clicks of your website, make it the button.
  2. Test one change at a time. Swap your banner or call to action, then compare next month's clicks.
  3. Compare people on your team. Filter by signature to see which layouts and titles get the most engagement.
  4. Set a monthly goal. The goal tracker shows your progress, for example 238 of 325 clicks.

A booking button is usually the most-clicked link in a signature. If you sell anything, add one and watch the “Clicks by link” report.

Frequently asked questions

What does email signature tracking measure?

SignTry counts clicks on the links in your signature, such as your website, social profiles and booking button, and shows them per month, per day, per link and per signature.

Can I set a click goal?

Yes. Set a monthly goal on the dashboard and a progress bar shows how close you are, for example 238 of 325 clicks.

Can I see analytics for each person on my team?

Yes. Pick a signature from the menu at the top of the dashboard to see that person’s clicks, or view the totals for everyone.

Are analytics included in every plan?

Analytics are included in every team plan, from 5 signatures up. See pricing.

Which time zone are clicks counted in?

Clicks are counted in UTC so every team member sees the same numbers.
