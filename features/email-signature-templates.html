What makes a good email signature template

A good template does three things: it tells people who you are, how to reach you, and what to do next. Everything else is decoration. When you pick one, check that it:

  1. Leads with your name. It should be the biggest, boldest line.
  2. Keeps contact details to two or three lines. Phone, email and website are usually enough.
  3. Uses one accent color. Match it to your logo or brand.
  4. Has room for one call to action, such as “Book a call” or a link to your listings.
  5. Stays under about 600 pixels wide, so it never wraps on a phone. Read more in our email signature size guide.

Tip: the simplest template is often the most professional. If you are unsure, pick a side-by-side layout with your photo, name, title and one link.
