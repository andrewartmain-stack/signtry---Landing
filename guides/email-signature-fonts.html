The best fonts for email signatures

The best font for an email signature is one that every recipient actually sees. Below are 12 web-safe fonts that display correctly in Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail, the right sizes to use, and how to get a handwritten signature font without it breaking.

Updated October 28, 2026 · 6 min read · By the SignTry team

Quick answer: Use a web-safe font such as Arial, Helvetica, Verdana, Georgia or Tahoma, at 14–16px for your name and 12–14px for everything else. Custom fonts, including handwritten signature fonts, usually fall back to a default in Gmail and Outlook, so add those as an image instead.

Why web-safe fonts matter in email signatures

Email apps do not download fonts the way websites do. If you use a font the recipient does not have installed, their email app quietly swaps it for a default, often Times New Roman, and your carefully designed signature looks broken. Web-safe fonts are installed on almost every computer and phone, so everyone sees what you see.

Apple Mail and the iPhone can show some web fonts; Gmail and Outlook on Windows generally cannot. Always set a fallback, for example font-family: Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif.

The best font size and style for an email signature

  • Name: 14–16px, bold.
  • Title, company and contact details: 12–14px, regular.
  • Disclaimers: 10–11px, gray.
  • One font, two weights. Bold for your name, regular for the rest. Two fonts at most.
  • Dark gray or your brand color for text, not pure black, for a softer look.

Fonts available in Gmail signatures

The Gmail signature editor offers a fixed list: Sans Serif, Serif, Fixed Width, Wide, Narrow, Comic Sans MS, Garamond, Georgia, Tahoma, Trebuchet MS and Verdana. Stick to these and your Gmail signature will look the same for everyone. See our Gmail signature guide to set it up.

Signature fonts that look handwritten

Searching for a font that looks like a signature? Script fonts such as Great Vibes, Dancing Script, Allura or Sacramento (all free on Google Fonts) look like real handwriting. The catch: they are not installed on your recipients' devices, so typed in a script font, your signature will show as plain text.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best font for an email signature?

Arial, Helvetica, Verdana, Georgia and Tahoma are the safest choices because they are installed on almost every device and display the same in Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail.

What font size should an email signature be?

Use 14–16px for your name and 12–14px for your title and contact details. Disclaimers can be 10–11px.

Can I use a custom or Google font in my email signature?

Only Apple Mail reliably shows web fonts. Gmail and Outlook replace them with a default, so always set a web-safe fallback such as Arial.

What font looks like a real signature?

Script fonts such as Great Vibes, Dancing Script, Allura and Sacramento look handwritten. Add them as an image, or use SignTry’s handwritten signature option, so they display everywhere.

Is Calibri a good font for email signatures?

Calibri looks good in Outlook on Windows, but it is not installed on every Mac or phone. Add Arial as a fallback.
