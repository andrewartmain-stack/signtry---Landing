How to change your signature in Yahoo Mail

Here is how to change your signature in Yahoo Mail, or add one for the first time, on a computer and in the Yahoo Mail app. It takes about a minute, and you can add formatting, links and images on desktop.

Updated October 28, 2026 · 4 min read · By the SignTry team

Quick answer: On a computer, click the Settings gear → More Settings → Writing email, turn on the Signature switch for your address, and type or paste your signature. Changes save automatically.

On this page

How to add a signature in Yahoo Mail on a computer

  1. Sign in to Yahoo Mail in your browser.
  2. Click the Settings gear icon at the top right.
  3. Click More Settings.
  4. Select Writing email.
  5. Under Signature, turn on the switch next to the email address you want.
  6. Type or paste your signature in the box. It is saved automatically; click Back to Inbox when you are done.

To change your signature later, follow the same steps and edit the text in the box. To remove it, turn the switch off.

How to change your signature in the Yahoo Mail app

  1. Open the Yahoo Mail app on iPhone or Android.
  2. Tap your profile icon or the menu, then Settings.
  3. Tap Signature.
  4. Turn the signature on for your account and edit the text. The default is “Sent from Yahoo Mail for iPhone” or “…for Android”.

The app signature is separate from the desktop one and is mostly plain text, so keep it short: your name, title and one link.

How to add links, images and formatting

The desktop signature box has a small toolbar for bold, italics, colors and links. To add an image, the most reliable way is to paste a signature whose images are hosted online. Images copied from your computer may not show for every recipient.

Yahoo signature not showing?

  • The switch is off. Each address in Writing email has its own on/off switch.
  • You are on the app. The app uses its own signature setting; set it there too.
  • Pasted as plain text. Paste with Ctrl+V or Cmd+V, not “paste as plain text”, to keep formatting.

Frequently asked questions

How do I change my signature in Yahoo Mail?

Click the Settings gear, More Settings, Writing email, then edit the text in the Signature box for your address. Changes save automatically.

How do I add a signature in Yahoo Mail on my phone?

In the Yahoo Mail app, tap your profile icon or menu, then Settings, Signature, and turn it on for your account.

Can I add a picture to my Yahoo Mail signature?

Yes, on desktop. Paste a signature with hosted images for the most reliable result; images from your computer may not show for everyone.

How do I remove “Sent from Yahoo Mail for iPhone”?

In the app, go to Settings, Signature, and either edit the text or turn the signature off.
