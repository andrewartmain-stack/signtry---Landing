How to change your email signature on iPhone

You can change your email signature on iPhone in about 30 seconds and finally replace “Sent from my iPhone”. This guide covers the iPhone Mail app, Outlook and Gmail on iPhone, and how to add an HTML signature to Apple Mail on a Mac.

Updated October 28, 2026 · 6 min read · By the SignTry team

Quick answer: Open Settings → Apps → Mail → Signature (on iOS 17 and earlier: Settings → Mail → Signature), delete “Sent from my iPhone”, and type or paste your new signature. It saves automatically.

How to change your email signature on iPhone Mail

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Scroll down and tap Apps, then Mail. (On iOS 17 or earlier, tap Mail directly.)
  3. Scroll to Composing and tap Signature.
  4. Choose All Accounts for one signature everywhere, or Per Account for a different one on each email address.
  5. Delete “Sent from my iPhone” and type or paste your signature. There is no save button; it saves as you type.

The same steps work on iPad, where the default text is “Sent from my iPad”.

How to keep bold text, colors and links

Select text in the signature box and tap BIU in the menu to make it bold, italic or underlined. To use a designed signature, open it on your iPhone (for example, email it to yourself), copy it, and paste it into the Signature field.

Lost the formatting after pasting? Shake your iPhone right away and tap Undo. iOS often strips styles in a second step, and undoing that step brings your colors and layout back.

Images in iPhone signatures are hit and miss: some recipients see them as attachments. A signature with hosted images, like the ones SignTry generates, avoids this.

How to change your signature in Outlook and Gmail on iPhone

If you use the Outlook or Gmail app instead of Apple Mail, the signature lives inside that app:

AppWhere to find the signature
Outlook for iPhoneTap your profile picture → Settings gear → Signature
Gmail for iPhoneTap the menu → Settings → your account → Signature settings → turn on Mobile signature
Yahoo Mail for iPhoneTap your profile icon → Settings → Signature. See our Yahoo Mail guide

How to add a signature in Apple Mail on Mac

  1. Open Mail and choose Mail → Settings (called Preferences on older macOS).
  2. Click Signatures.
  3. Select an account in the left column and click +.
  4. Name the signature and type or paste it on the right.
  5. Uncheck Always match my default message font to keep your own fonts and colors.
  6. Use the Choose Signature menu to make it the default for that account.

How to add an HTML signature to Apple Mail

Pasting a designed signature into the Signatures window works for most people. If yours loses its layout, you can install the HTML directly:

  1. Create a placeholder signature in Mail → Settings → Signatures and quit Mail.
  2. In Finder, choose Go → Go to Folder and open ~/Library/Mail/, then the newest V folder (for example V10), then MailData/Signatures.
  3. Open the newest .mailsignature file in TextEdit, keep the header lines at the top, and replace everything below them with your signature HTML.
  4. Save, then select the file, choose File → Get Info and tick Locked so Mail does not overwrite it.
  5. Reopen Mail. Your HTML signature appears in the Signatures window.

Frequently asked questions

How do I remove “Sent from my iPhone”?

Go to Settings, Apps, Mail, Signature (or Settings, Mail, Signature on older iOS) and delete the text. Leave it empty for no signature, or type your own.

Why did my iPhone signature lose its formatting?

iOS sometimes strips styles after you paste. Shake your iPhone straight after pasting and tap Undo to restore the formatting.

Can I have a different signature for each email account on iPhone?

Yes. In the Signature settings, choose Per Account and enter a signature under each address.

Can I add a logo to my iPhone email signature?

You can paste a signature that contains a logo, but embedded images often arrive as attachments. Signatures with hosted images, like SignTry signatures, display more reliably.

Does my iPhone signature sync to my Mac?

No. iPhone Mail and Mac Mail signatures are set separately, so add your signature on each device.
