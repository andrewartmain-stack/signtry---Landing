Email signature size: the right dimensions for every part

The ideal email signature size is no more than 600 pixels wide and roughly 100 to 200 pixels tall. Below are the exact dimensions for your photo, logo, banner and social icons, plus the file sizes and formats that display reliably in every inbox.

Updated October 28, 2026 · 5 min read · By the SignTry team

Quick answer: Keep your email signature under 600px wide and about 100–200px tall. Use a headshot around 80–120px square, a logo 30–60px tall and up to 200px wide, a banner up to 600×150px, and social icons 16–24px. Upload images at twice the display size so they look sharp on high-resolution screens.

Overall email signature dimensions

Most email apps show messages in a column 600 to 800 pixels wide, and phones are narrower still. A signature up to 600px wide fits everywhere without wrapping. Aim for 100–200px tall: enough for a photo, four lines of text and a row of icons. Anything taller starts to feel like an advert.

Email signature photo size

Display your headshot at 80–120px square. Upload it at twice that size (for example 240×240px) so it stays crisp on Retina and high-DPI screens. Use a square crop with your face centered; SignTry crops it around your face automatically and lets you choose square, rounded or circle shapes.

Email signature logo size

A logo 30–60px tall and no wider than 200px is easy to recognize without overpowering your name. SignTry shows logos about 28px tall next to your details. Use a transparent PNG so it looks right on light and dark themes, and trim any empty space around it before uploading.

Email signature banner size

Banners promote an event, offer or new product under your signature. Keep them at most 600px wide and 150px tall; 600×100px is a common choice. Put the message in the image itself in large text, since many people only glance at it.

File size and image formats

  • Keep each image under about 100KB, and the whole signature light, so emails load fast on mobile data.
  • Use JPG for photos and PNG for logos and icons.
  • Avoid SVG. Gmail and Outlook do not display SVG images in emails.
  • Animated GIFs: classic Outlook for Windows shows only the first frame, so make sure it works as a still image.
  • Host your images. Images embedded in the email often show up as attachments. SignTry hosts them for you.

Font size and signature length

Use 14–16px for your name and 12–14px for everything else, in a web-safe font like Arial, Helvetica, Verdana or Georgia. Keep the text to four or five lines. Gmail also limits signatures to 10,000 characters, which a signature with lots of formatting can hit; if Gmail says your signature is too long, simplify it or use hosted images.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best size for an email signature?

No more than 600 pixels wide and about 100–200 pixels tall, with four or five lines of text.

What size should my email signature photo be?

Display it at 80–120 pixels square and upload it at twice that size, for example 240×240 pixels.

What size should a logo be in an email signature?

About 30–60 pixels tall and up to 200 pixels wide. Use a transparent PNG.

What is the best image size for an Outlook email signature?

The same as other apps: keep the whole signature under 600 pixels wide, logos up to 200 pixels wide and photos around 100 pixels square. Avoid SVG images, which Outlook does not show.

Why is my email signature too long for Gmail?

Gmail allows up to 10,000 characters, including hidden formatting code. Simplify the layout or use hosted images instead of embedded ones.
