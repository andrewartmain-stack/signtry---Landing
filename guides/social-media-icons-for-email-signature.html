Social media icons for your email signature

Social media icons for your email signature turn every email into a path to your LinkedIn, Instagram or portfolio. This guide covers which icons to include for your job, the right size and style, and how to add them in Gmail, Outlook and SignTry.

Updated October 28, 2026 · 5 min read · By the SignTry team

Quick answer: Add two to four social media icons that are relevant to your work, at 16–24px, in your brand color or each network’s official color. Link each icon to your profile, not the network’s homepage, and use hosted PNG images, since Gmail and Outlook do not display SVG.

Which social media icons should you include?

More icons is not better. Pick the profiles a contact would actually want to visit:

Your roleIcons to include
Sales, recruiting, consultingLinkedIn, plus a booking link such as Calendly
Real estate agentsInstagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube for property tours
Designers and creativesBehance or Dribbble, Instagram, LinkedIn
DevelopersGitHub, LinkedIn, X
Creators and marketersInstagram, TikTok, YouTube
Local businessesFacebook, Instagram, WhatsApp
Students and job seekersLinkedIn, plus a portfolio or GitHub if relevant

Only link profiles you keep up to date and professional. An empty or personal profile does more harm than no icon at all.

The right size and style for social icons

  • Size: 16–24px is standard; 20px is a safe default. Upload at 2× (40px) for sharp results on Retina screens.
  • Spacing: leave 6–10px between icons so they are easy to tap on a phone.
  • Color: use official brand colors, or make every icon one color that matches your signature.
  • Format: PNG, hosted online. SVG and icon fonts do not show in Gmail or Outlook.
  • Alt text: add “LinkedIn”, “Instagram” and so on, so people with images turned off still know where the link goes.

How to add social icons manually in Gmail or Outlook

  1. Download each icon as a PNG, about 40×40px, and upload it somewhere public, such as your website.
  2. Open your signature settings (Gmail or Outlook).
  3. Click the image button in the signature editor and insert the icon by its URL.
  4. Select the icon, click the link button, and paste your profile URL.
  5. Repeat for each icon, then save.

It works, but it is slow and the icons often end up different sizes. A generator does it in one step.

Brand rules for social media logos

Networks are strict about how their logos are used. Use the official icon unchanged: do not stretch it, rotate it, add effects or redraw it. You can usually show it in the network's color, or in a single solid color such as black, white or your brand color. The icons on this page are the official brand marks.

Frequently asked questions

What size should social media icons be in an email signature?

16–24 pixels, with 20 pixels a safe default. Upload them at twice the size so they look sharp on high-resolution screens.

How many social media icons should I put in my email signature?

Two to four. Choose the profiles that matter for your work and keep them up to date.

Why are my social media icons not showing?

Usually because they are SVG files or embedded images. Use hosted PNG images, which display in Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail.

Where can I get free social media icons for my email signature?

Use each network’s official brand assets, or a generator like SignTry that adds correctly sized, hosted icons for you.

Should social icons be in color or black and white?

Either works. Official colors are recognizable; a single color matching your brand looks more refined. Do not change the shape of the logos.
