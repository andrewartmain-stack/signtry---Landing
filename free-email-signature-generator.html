Buyers save agent emails for months. A realtor email signature should make you easy to call and easy to trust.

Best practices

What to include in a professional email signature

The best email signatures are short. Aim for four or five lines of text, one image and one clear next step.

Think of your signature as a business card that is attached to every email you send. People should be able to see who you are, what you do and how to reach you in a couple of seconds.

Personal email signatures can be lighter: your name, one link and maybe a short quote. Business email signatures should stay consistent across the whole company.

  • Full name and job title. Add credentials such as MBA or PhD and your pronouns if you use them.
  • Company and website. Show the domain without “https://www.”, it is easier to read.
  • One or two contact methods. Phone and email are enough. Every number should be clickable on mobile.
  • A professional photo. Emails with a face get recognized and answered faster.
  • Two to four social icons. Only the profiles you actually keep up to date.
  • One call to action. Book a call, see listings, read the case study. One button, not five.
  • Avoid long quotes, more than two fonts, images of text, and your email address written twice.
Size guide

Email signature size: image, logo and photo dimensions

The right email signature dimensions keep it sharp on retina screens and stop it from breaking on phones. Use images at twice the size they are shown.

Install

How to add your signature to Gmail, Outlook and Apple Mail

Click Copy signature above, then follow the steps for your email app.

How to add a signature in Gmail

  1. Open Gmail on a computer and click the gear icon, then See all settings.
  2. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new.
  3. Name it, then paste your signature into the box.
  4. Under Signature defaults, choose it for new emails and replies.
  5. Scroll down and click Save Changes.

How to create an email signature in Outlook

  1. In the new Outlook or Outlook on the web, open Settings, then Accounts and Signatures.
  2. Click New signature, give it a name and paste your signature.
  3. Choose it as the default for new messages and replies, then click Save.
  4. Classic Outlook for Windows: File › Options › Mail › Signatures › New, paste, then click OK.

How to add a signature in Apple Mail

  1. Open Mail, then Mail › Settings › Signatures.
  2. Choose your account and click +.
  3. Untick Always match my default message font.
  4. Paste your signature into the right-hand box. It saves automatically.

How to add a signature in iPhone Mail

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone and tap Copy signature.
  2. Go to Settings › Apps › Mail › Signature.
  3. Delete the existing text and paste.
  4. If the layout looks plain, shake your iPhone and tap Undo Change Attributes.

How to add a signature in Yahoo Mail

  1. Click Settings, then More Settings.
  2. Open Writing email and turn on the signature for your account.
  3. Paste your signature into the box. Yahoo saves it automatically.

How to add a signature in Thunderbird

  1. Click Copy HTML above.
  2. Open Account Settings and select your email address.
  3. Tick Use HTML next to Signature text.
  4. Paste the HTML code into the box and close the tab.

FAQ

Email signature questions

Is this email signature generator really free?

Yes. You can pick any template, fill in your details and copy the signature as many times as you like. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no trial that runs out.

Why do I paste a link to my photo instead of uploading it?

Email clients only show images that are hosted online. Gmail and Outlook block or strip images embedded inside the signature, so your photo has to live at a public link, for example on your website or an image host that gives a direct link ending in .jpg or .png.

Will my signature work in Outlook and Gmail?

Yes. Every template is built with email-safe tables and inline styles, the same way professional email newsletters are built, so it looks right in Gmail, Outlook (new, classic and web), Apple Mail, iPhone Mail, Yahoo Mail and Thunderbird.

Gmail says my signature is too long. What do I do?

Gmail allows up to 10,000 characters of signature code. The generator shows how many characters your signature uses. If you are over the limit, remove a social icon, shorten the disclaimer, or use a simpler template.

How do I add pronouns to my email signature?

Type them in the Pronouns field in the Details step, for example she/her or they/them. They appear in brackets right after your name, in a smaller, lighter style.

What should a professional email signature include?

Your full name, job title, company, one or two ways to contact you, and your website. Add a photo, a license or bar number if your profession requires it, a few social icons and one call-to-action button. Keep it to four or five lines of text.

What size should an email signature be?

Keep the whole signature under 600 pixels wide, ideally 300 to 500 pixels. Show your photo at 70 to 100 pixels, using an image of about 200 by 200 pixels so it stays sharp on high-resolution screens, and keep each image under 100 KB.
