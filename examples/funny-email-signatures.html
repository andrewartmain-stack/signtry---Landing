Funny email signatures and email signature quotes

A funny email signature or a well-chosen quote gives your emails personality and makes people remember you. Here are more than 40 humorous email signatures, witty taglines and quotes you can copy, plus how to use them without looking unprofessional.

Updated October 28, 2026 · 5 min read · By the SignTry team

Quick answer: Add humor as one short tagline under your contact details, in a smaller, lighter font. Keep your name, title and contact information first, and use a funny email signature only for casual or internal email.

Email signature quotes

A short quote can say what you stand for. Choose one that is accurately attributed and relevant to your work:

  • “The best way to predict the future is to invent it.” — Alan Kay
  • “Stay hungry, stay foolish.” — Steve Jobs, quoting the Whole Earth Catalog
  • “The only way to do great work is to love what you do.” — Steve Jobs
  • “Talk is cheap. Show me the code.” — Linus Torvalds
  • “Premature optimization is the root of all evil.” — Donald Knuth
  • “Make it work, make it right, make it fast.” — Kent Beck

Many quotes online are misattributed, for example most “Einstein” and “Mark Twain” quotes. If you are not sure who said it, use it without a name or pick a different line.

How to keep a funny email signature professional

  1. Information first. Name, title and contact details come before the joke.
  2. One line only. A tagline, not a stand-up set.
  3. Make it smaller and lighter than the rest of your signature.
  4. Avoid politics, religion and anything about other people.
  5. Keep a second, serious signature for clients and formal email.

Frequently asked questions

Is a funny email signature unprofessional?

Not if it is short, harmless and comes after your contact details. Keep a serious version for clients, job applications and formal emails.

Where should a quote go in an email signature?

At the bottom, after your name and contact details, in a smaller and lighter font than the rest of the signature.

What is a good funny email signature for work?

A short, self-deprecating tagline about your job, such as “Replying at the speed of coffee” or “Professional overthinker of subject lines”.

Should I put a quote in my email signature?

Only if it is short, relevant to your work and correctly attributed. Many popular quotes are misattributed, so double-check the source.
