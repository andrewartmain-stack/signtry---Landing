Teacher email signature examples

A clear teacher email signature saves parents time: they see your name, grade or subject, school and the best way to reach you, without digging through old emails. Here are 8 teacher email signature examples to copy, plus what to include and what to avoid.

Updated October 28, 2026 · 5 min read · By the SignTry team

Quick answer: A teacher email signature should include your name (as students call you, such as “Ms. Rivera”), your grade or subject, your school, a phone number or extension, and when you are available, such as office hours. Add a link to your class page if you have one.

What to include in a teacher email signature

  • Your name the way families know you: “Ms. Rivera” or “Mr. Thompson”, with your full name if you like.
  • Grade level or subject, such as “3rd Grade” or “AP Biology”.
  • School name, and district if you write to families across several schools.
  • Phone and extension, or a note that email is the best way to reach you.
  • Availability: office hours or when you reply to email.
  • Class website or learning platform link.
  • Pronouns, optional.

Many schools and districts have rules for staff email signatures, for example no personal social media or quotes. Check your school’s guidelines before you add anything beyond the basics.

Tips for teacher emails to parents

  1. Set expectations. A line such as “I reply within one school day” prevents follow-up emails.
  2. Make booking easy. A “Book a conference” link saves a long back-and-forth.
  3. List languages you speak, so families know they can write in their own.
  4. Keep it short. Parents read your emails on their phones.

Frequently asked questions

What should a teacher put in their email signature?

Your name as students know you, grade or subject, school, a phone number or extension, and your availability such as office hours. A class website link is a helpful extra.

Should teachers include a quote in their email signature?

Usually not in emails to parents and staff. Many districts discourage quotes in staff signatures, so check your school’s guidelines.

Can a whole school use the same signature design?

Yes. With SignTry you design one signature and generate one for every staff member from a CSV, each with their own name and subject.

Should I include my personal phone number?

No. Use your school phone and extension, or say that email is the best way to reach you.
