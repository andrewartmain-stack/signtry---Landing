5 rules every good email signature follows

  1. Name first, biggest. Everything else supports it.
  2. Four or five lines. If it scrolls, it is too long.
  3. One call to action. A booking link beats five social icons.
  4. One accent color, taken from your logo.
  5. Under 600px wide, so it fits on a phone. See our size guide.

Frequently asked questions

What is an email signature block?

The few lines of text at the end of an email that identify the sender: usually name, job title, company and contact details.

What should a professional email signature include?

Your name, job title, company, one or two contact details and one link. A photo or logo is optional.

What is a good personal email signature?

Your name, what you do, and a link to your best work or LinkedIn. Keep it to two or three lines.

Should I put a quote in my email signature?

In business email, usually not. A short tagline about what you do is more useful than a quote.
