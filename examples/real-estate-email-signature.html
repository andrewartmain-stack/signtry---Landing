Real estate email signature examples for agents and brokers

Your real estate email signature works like a business card on every email you send to buyers, sellers and other agents. These realtor email signature examples show what to include, from your headshot and brokerage to your license number and listings link.

Updated October 28, 2026 · 6 min read · By the SignTry team

Quick answer: A real estate email signature should include your headshot, name and title, brokerage name and logo, license number where required, phone, and a link to your listings or booking page. Social icons for Instagram and Facebook are a bonus.

License numbers and compliance

Advertising rules for real estate are set by each state, and many count email as advertising. Common requirements include showing your brokerage name and, in some states, your license number. Rules vary, so check your state real estate commission and your broker’s policy. If you are a member of the National Association of REALTORS®, use the REALTOR® trademark correctly, in capitals with the ® symbol.

Not sure what your state requires? Ask your managing broker. They review marketing materials regularly and can confirm in a minute.

Frequently asked questions

What should a realtor put in their email signature?

A headshot, your name and title, brokerage name and logo, license number where required, mobile phone, and a link to your listings or booking page.

Do I need my license number in my email signature?

It depends on your state. Many states treat email as advertising and require the brokerage name, and some require the license number. Check your state real estate commission.

Should real estate agents include a photo in their email signature?

Yes. A professional headshot helps clients recognize and trust you, and it is standard in real estate.
