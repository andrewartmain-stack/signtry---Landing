Christmas and holiday email signature ideas

A Christmas or holiday email signature is a small, festive touch on every email you send in December. Below are holiday and Thanksgiving email signature examples, greetings you can copy, banner ideas, and how to announce office closures without anyone missing it.

Updated October 28, 2026 · 5 min read · By the SignTry team

Quick answer: Add one short seasonal greeting (for example “Wishing you a happy holiday season”) and, if needed, your office closure dates under your normal signature. Use an inclusive greeting for clients, keep any banner under 600×150px, and switch back to your usual signature in early January.

Holiday email signature greetings to copy

Inclusive greetings (best for clients)

  • Wishing you a happy holiday season and a wonderful new year.
  • Warm wishes for the holidays and a bright 2027.
  • Season’s greetings from all of us at [Company].
  • Thank you for a great year. Happy holidays!
  • Wishing you peace and joy this holiday season.

Merry Christmas greetings

  • Merry Christmas and a happy new year!
  • Wishing you a merry Christmas and a joyful new year.
  • Merry Christmas from the [Company] team.
  • Have a cozy Christmas and a great start to the new year.

Fun holiday lines

  • Currently powered by cookies and hot chocolate.
  • Replying between gingerbread batches.
  • My inbox is on the nice list. Is yours?
  • Out of office, in a holiday sweater.

Thanksgiving email signature ideas

  • Happy Thanksgiving! Grateful for clients like you.
  • Wishing you a Thanksgiving full of good food and great company.
  • Thankful for your partnership this year.
  • Out for Thanksgiving, back Monday with leftovers.

How to announce office closures in your signature

Your signature is seen on every email, so it is the perfect place for holiday hours. Keep it to one line with exact dates, and point to a backup contact for urgent requests:

Closure line
We are closed Dec 24 – Jan 1. For urgent help, email support@company.com.

Pair it with an out-of-office auto-reply for the days you are actually away.

Holiday banners and images for email signatures

  • Size: up to 600×150px displayed, uploaded at 2× for sharp screens. See our size guide.
  • Animated GIFs are fun, but classic Outlook for Windows shows only the first frame, so make sure that frame works on its own.
  • Host the image instead of attaching it, or it may show as an attachment.
  • Emoji such as 🎄 ❄️ 🎁 are the lightest way to add color, and they work in every modern email app.

When to add and remove your holiday signature

Switch to your holiday signature in early December (or the week before Thanksgiving) and back to your normal one in the first week of January. In SignTry, keep your holiday version as a second signature and copy whichever one fits the season; for teams, update everyone at once by generating from a CSV.

Frequently asked questions

What should I put in my Christmas email signature?

Your normal signature plus one short greeting, such as “Merry Christmas and a happy new year!”, and your office closure dates if they apply.

Should I say Merry Christmas or Happy Holidays in a work email?

For clients and mixed audiences, “Happy holidays” or “Season’s greetings” is the safer, inclusive choice. Use “Merry Christmas” with people you know celebrate it.

Can I add a GIF to my holiday email signature?

Yes, but classic Outlook for Windows shows only the first frame, so choose a GIF whose first frame looks good on its own.

When should I remove my holiday email signature?

In the first week of January, or the day after the holiday it mentions.

What is a good Thanksgiving email signature?

A short line of thanks, such as “Happy Thanksgiving! Grateful for clients like you,” under your usual signature.
